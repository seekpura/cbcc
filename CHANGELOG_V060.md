# CBCC v0.6.0 — Capability-Complete Rebase

## Why this release exists
The v0.5.x line had accumulated substantial strategy, evidence, platform, listing and production-control depth, but the last mile was still uneven: creative directions were not reliably generated when absent, RACE semantics were wrong in one runtime path, Surface planning was too generic, and final per-asset prompts compressed too much upstream reasoning. v0.6.0 fixes the **capability itself** rather than adding another validation layer.

## 1. Correct Campaign RACE
Canonical campaign journey is now:
`PLAN → REACH → ACT → CONVERT → ENGAGE`.
Asset decision intents map to the relevant stage; the old Research/Action/Communication/Evaluation interpretation is no longer authoritative.

## 2. Product Visual Intelligence
New runtime builds an SKU-bound visual truth layer covering known geometry/structure, material/finish, transparency/reflectivity, label/identity faces, source masters, canonical view needs, capture risks and reconstruction permissions. Unknowns remain unknown.

## 3. Foundation and Creative Anchors
Foundation assets preserve product identity. Creative Anchors preserve selected lighting/material/scene/keyframe language. They support continuity without forcing identical layout.

## 4. Creative Opportunity Universe → Direction Synthesis / Council
When explicit directions are absent, runtime now searches **6–12 mechanism-level opportunities first**. The universe is scored from the current SKU, Product Visual Intelligence, atomic decision intents, Surface roles, material/scale/use/risk/system signals and reference whitespace/cliches. Mechanisms can include Product Identity Geometry, Evidence Relationship, Human Decisive Moment, Material/Optical Behavior, Scale/Space, Process/Sequence, Risk Boundary, System Relationship, Package/Contents Reveal and remote-domain spatial grammar. Council then synthesizes **3–5 materially different directions** from the highest-fit mechanisms. Stable route labels remain only for downstream compatibility; they are not the visual style library.

## 5. Dynamic Surface and Information Assetization
Surface planning now treats Listing Primary, PDP Hero, Gallery, Longform/A+, Video, Short Video, Creator, Live, Paid, Social, Store, Landing, Comparison, Specification, FAQ, UGC, Post-purchase and CRM as candidates whose need is adjudicated. Information is first assigned to IMAGE / NATIVE_TEXT / HYBRID / SOURCE_VIEW / NOT_NEEDED / OFF_DOMAIN_HANDOFF.

## 6. Product Presence and Copy Budget
Product appearance is deliberate instead of mandatory. Each asset carries product-presence intent plus a Surface-specific semantic copy budget; exact platform character limits are never invented when the current rule does not supply them.

## 7. Per-asset visual differentiation
Each decision job receives an expression variant and spatial rhythm tied to its shopper task. Examples include Identity Hero, Context Entry, Evidence Editorial, Decisive Action, Mechanism Compare, Scale Measure, Fit Guide, Material Macro and Relationship Diagram. One design system is therefore a coherence layer, not a same-layout template.

## 8. Deep Design Card and Canonical Prompt
Every activated asset now compiles:
- shopper question / decision intent / barrier / belief transition / RACE stage;
- why the asset exists and incremental information over adjacent assets;
- creative direction + visual expression variant;
- visual narrative + decisive moment;
- subject / action / environment / props / human role;
- composition, spatial rhythm, camera, lens/projection, crop, product orientation and safe zone;
- lighting, shadow/reflection, DOF, CMF and material behavior;
- primary/secondary/proof information hierarchy, locked visible copy and language contract;
- Surface contract, information representation and Product Presence;
- real source masters, product-fidelity boundary, PVI/Anchor references;
- editable and frozen layers;
- measurement hypothesis.

The output is a full Chinese prompt and a full English prompt, plus Machine Visual Contract, Renderer Recipe and QA Target. Exact approved visible copy is locked and should be deterministically composed when precision matters.

## 9. Asset package compatibility
The canonical `00–11` package is unchanged. v0.6.0 adds:
- `12_design_card.json`
- `13_prompt_contract.json`
No historical production file is removed.

## 10. Provider production language
Provider Jobs now receive Canonical Prompt Contract, Renderer Recipe and QA Target together with package/master/photography/layout bindings. Product-master fidelity, semantic editable/frozen layers and local revision protections from v0.5.14 remain in force.

## 11. Paid / Creator / Video / Live
Paid is now an independent Campaign Architecture, not a resized PDP image. It contains objective, audience state, consumer tension, offer, proof mechanism, landing handoff, at least two materially different concepts and test hypotheses. Creator/Video/Live use native hook → reveal/demo/proof → objection/belief close → CTA/reset logic plus rights/usage contracts.

## 12. Preserved systems
No rebase regression is intended for Product Truth, Market/Competitor/Search, SKU/Offer/Economics, Positioning/Message, Listing/Final Copy, Platform/Locale, Evidence reconciliation, Product Master binding, Provider Result, Review, Local Revision or Learning.
