# CBCC v0.6.36 — Seven-Tool Capability Continuity + Pricing/Economics Integrity

v0.6.36 restores the commercial brain as a first-class peer of the visual/production system. It preserves v0.6.30–v0.6.35 Agent Art Direction, Model-Native Prompt, Production Parameter Closure, Asset Universe, multi-Hero and reference-guided native product generation.

## Seven first-class tools

1. Market Research
2. Product Strategy — SKU + Pricing + Economics + Offer
3. Listing Creative
4. Product Image
5. Product Video
6. Advertising Optimization
7. Review Analysis

Each capability is release-checked as `Runtime entry → real consumer → business output → human delivery → plugin top-level activation`. File presence alone does not count as continuity.

## Pricing / Economics Integrity

A new strict `pricing_economics_authority` materializes:
- Market Price Bands and Price Valleys
- Price × Configuration Matrix
- SKU Roles
- explicit-input Unit Economics
- Contribution Waterfall and pre-ad Contribution Margin
- Break-even CPA
- Break-even CPC only when click→order CVR is explicitly supplied
- Break-even ROAS on the same per-order revenue basis
- Offer / Bundle / Discount Boundary
- Pricing Decision + WHY, kept Agent-authored

Missing cost components, fee values, rate bases and CVR are unresolved inputs. They are never silently defaulted to zero. Explicit zero remains a valid supplied value.

## Downstream commercial consumers

- Product Strategy receives the pricing/economics authority as part of the economics object consumed by Master Strategy.
- Listing receives pricing/offer context and economic boundaries without forcing price copy into every field or asset.
- Ads receives break-even CPA/CPC/ROAS floors for economics-aware diagnosis.
- Image/Video may consume offer/economic context when it serves a purchase decision; pricing does not become mandatory visible copy.

## Human delivery

The Chinese-first `index.html` workspace gains a Commercial Brain section exposing:
- all seven tools with Runtime entry, real consumers, business outputs and delivery surface;
- market price bands / valleys / price-configuration matrix;
- SKU roles;
- contribution and break-even economics;
- offer/discount boundaries;
- Pricing Decision + WHY;
- explicit missing-input list.

## Provider cleanup

Pixel-layer manifests now activate only for explicit `EXACT_PIXEL_COMPOSITE` / `PIXEL_COMPOSITE_EXPLICIT`. Legacy `LOCKED_PIXEL` no longer implicitly creates an exact-pixel layer manifest.

Release acceptance rerun after repairing the migration script quoting issue found by the first gate.
