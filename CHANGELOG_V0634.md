# CBCC v0.6.34 — Native-Reference Authority Cleanup

This is a cleanup release on top of v0.6.33. It does not change the newly established business semantics; it removes contradictory historical wording discovered by reading back the actually installed plugin.

- Removes duplicate `Dynamic Asset Universe + Native Product Reference` wrapper section.
- Removes the historical `LOCKED_SOURCE_COMPOSITE` background/composite-default authority sentence from the active root Skill.
- Removes ambiguous wrapper wording that could imply all product images are merely ordinary design references.
- Keeps the v0.6.33 rule: when Product Presence requires the real product to be visible, real product images are strong model Reference Inputs and the product is natively rendered in the complete final image under `REFERENCE_GUIDED_NATIVE_LOCK`.
- Keeps `EXACT_PIXEL_COMPOSITE` explicit-only for a user requirement that original source pixels remain immutable.
- Keeps the unlimited Dynamic Asset Universe, multiple Hero/benefit/ad variants, 20+ testing/rotation candidates, and operational pools introduced in v0.6.33.
- Keeps v0.6.32 production-parameter closure and v0.6.31/v0.6.30 prompt/Agent Art Direction authority.
