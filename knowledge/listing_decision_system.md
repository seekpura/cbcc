# Listing as a Decision System

A listing is not a keyword container and not a repeated ad.

Search layer establishes relevance. Product card / title / primary visual establish exact identity and one reason to click. PDP then sequences relevance, proof, specifications, trust, risk reduction and action. FAQ / usage / package / compatibility content prevents avoidable returns and customer-service load.

Each field needs a distinct job, source/claim lineage and rationale. Repetition is acceptable only when deliberately reinforcing a high-priority belief.

## Expectation management
Package, usage, size, compatibility and variant clarity are conversion assets when they prevent uncertainty. They are also return-prevention assets after purchase. Treat recurring feedback on these topics as a route back to the responsible listing field, not as a reason to redesign unrelated brand/visual layers.

## Transcreation
Preserve product truth, numbers, claim strength and warning boundaries while adapting sentence order, keyword placement, CTA and information density to the local language and platform. Literal translation is not the default professional method.
