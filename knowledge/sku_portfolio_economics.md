# SKU Portfolio Economics

Portfolio strategy is a system of differentiated jobs, not a list of profitable products.

A SKU earns a role through a combination of buyer demand, unit contribution, channel job, lifecycle job and differentiation from adjacent SKUs. The same SKU may play different roles by channel or campaign, but those roles should be explicit.

## Overlap / Cannibalization

Review adjacent SKUs for shared buyer job, segment, configuration, channel, product family, role and price proximity. Deterministic overlap signals are prioritization aids only. Actual cannibalization requires behavioral evidence such as switching, incremental lift or cohort change.

## Trade-up

Each price step needs a distinct value reason. If the only difference is price, flag the step for redesign rather than inventing a premium story.

## Shared growth pool

Coupons, affiliate commission and paid media consume the same contribution pool. Portfolio decisions must compare contribution after offer costs, not GMV alone.

## Lifecycle

Acquisition, AOV, premiumization and retention can require different offers. Bundle and replenishment logic should be evaluated with attach/repeat, logistics, return and inventory evidence.
