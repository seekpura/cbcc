# Multi-category Commerce Specificity

## Principle
A cross-border commerce runtime must reuse decision architecture, not reuse one category's asset sequence, proof logic, or photography recipe.

## Runtime requirements
- Infer a coarse category family from explicit target/product category and product type only; never infer product facts or claims from category.
- Convert shopper questions into decision intents such as identity, size/capacity, fit/wear, material/sensory, closure/seal, storage/stacking, care, compatibility, proof and usage.
- Surface selection is a commercial decision: choose the surface that best resolves the intent among the surfaces requested by the project.
- Platform packs define evidence/submission readiness. Missing verified platform capability must create a research boundary, not delete an otherwise necessary strategic surface.
- Repeated questions with the same decision intent on the same surface should merge when one asset can answer them without losing material information.
- Category photography is a planning hypothesis until real-product prelight locks focal length, distance, height, exposure and material response.

## Category differences that must remain visible
- Beauty/personal care: package/formula/approved-proof truth, routine relevance, truthful skin/texture, claim boundary.
- Fashion/accessories: silhouette, on-body scale, fit/adjustment, capacity, textile/hardware detail, weather and care boundary.
- Home/durables: size/capacity, structure, closure/interface, material, stacking/storage, compatibility, maintenance.
- Food/beverage: package identity, ingredient/nutrition/allergen or certification facts when supplied, serving/preparation, pack size and storage.

## Anti-template check
If materially different categories produce the same visual territory, same surface sequence, same lens recommendation and same proof logic without product-specific reasons, treat that as strategy homogenization and revise the runtime rather than decorating the output differently.
