# Creative & Photography Strategy

Visual direction must answer a commercial question. Define style, composition, CMF, lighting, lens, working distance, camera height/angle, focus plane, depth of field, product scale, copy-safe zones, shadow/specular behavior, color fidelity, scene, props, model casting, wardrobe/hair/makeup and platform crop. Every major choice states WHY it improves identification, comprehension, proof, trust or desire.
