# Competitor Intelligence: Structure → Message → Expectation

Market rows describe structure: seller concentration, price, configuration and efficiency. Competitor Listing observations add what the market communicates; review themes add what buyers report after exposure/purchase/use.

Use the three layers together:

- **Structure:** who sells, at what price/configuration, with what apparent sales efficiency.
- **Message/Offer/Proof:** what competitors explicitly foreground on each surface.
- **Customer Voice:** repeated labelled expectations, objections, satisfaction and confusion themes.

A useful opportunity requires overlap between truthful own-product fit and a buyer decision need. "Competitors do not say X" is not enough; they may omit X because buyers do not care. "Competitors receive complaints about Y" is not enough; the own product must actually resolve Y and be able to prove/communicate it.

Prefer source-level observations with date/platform/locale. Keep stale or mixed-market evidence visibly bounded.
