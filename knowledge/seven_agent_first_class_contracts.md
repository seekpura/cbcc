# Seven first-class commerce contracts

CBCC treats the seven reference-agent responsibilities as stable orchestration contracts over the deeper runtime, rather than duplicating shallow calculators.

- Market Research: deterministic market/profile/concentration/price/configuration/competitor/keyword/VOC.
- Product Strategy: evidence-graded value ladder, SKU/offer economics, positioning and tradeoff.
- Listing Creative: search/keyword architecture, native field jobs, fact/claim trace, transcreation/back-translation, final-copy release.
- Product Image: PVI → opportunity universe → direction council → dynamic surface/assets → Design Card/canonical prompt.
- Product Video: script/storyboard/shot timing → AI-video/VO/subtitle/cover/music-rights/CTA/landing.
- Advertising Optimization: traffic→click→PDP→economics diagnosis, P0/P1/P2, attribution, incrementality boundary.
- Review Analysis: eight classes, misunderstanding, defect-vs-communication, fixability/praise, reconciliation, local feedback routing.
