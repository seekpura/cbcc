# Creative Direction → Surface → Per-Asset Prompt System

## Authority purpose
A commerce visual system must produce **coherence without sameness**. The selected direction defines common principles; every asset still derives its own composition, camera/view, product role, evidence plane, copy density, human/context role and surface behavior from the shopper decision it must resolve.

## Search before selection — Creative Opportunity Universe
- Before creating directions, compile **6–12 mechanism-level Creative Opportunities** from the current SKU: product identity geometry, evidence relationships, human decisive moments, material/optical behavior, scale/space calibration, process/sequence, risk/boundary, system/compatibility, package/contents reveal, information hierarchy, category whitespace and remote-domain spatial grammar as applicable.
- Score mechanisms from Product Visual Intelligence, atomic decision intents, Surface roles, current evidence, material/scale/use/risk/system signals and declared reference whitespace/cliches.
- Borrow mechanisms, never copy a finished reference composition.
- Only after this search, synthesize **3–5 materially different directions**. Stable downstream route IDs are compatibility tags, not a fixed style library.
- A meaningful direction changes at least four of: buyer entry, creative thesis, composition grammar, product role, image language, proof mechanism, verbal thesis, color world.

## Direction council
Evaluate product truth fit, buyer-decision fit, platform/surface fit, culture/localization fit, differentiation, production feasibility and decision coverage. Select a coherent system, then allow decision-specific secondary route influences when proof, human action or material study needs a different visual grammar.

## Product Visual Intelligence
Compile known product geometry, structure, material, finish, transparency/reflectivity, label/printed faces, canonical views, capture risks and source masters before downstream visual production. Unknown properties stay unknown. Real product masters outrank references or model reconstruction.

## Foundation vs Creative Anchors
- Foundation Assets answer **who/what the product is**.
- Creative Anchors answer **how this selected visual system behaves**.
- Typical anchor roles are Hero Keyframe, Lighting Reference, Material Response and Scene Language, but create only those required by the current project.
- Anchors improve continuity; they never force all assets into one layout.

## Dynamic Surface Architecture
Asset/surface count is a result of shopper decisions, evidence and platform roles—not a quota. Candidate expressions include search/listing, hero, gallery, longform/A+, specification/comparison, video, creator/live, paid/social/store, FAQ, CRM and post-purchase.

Before creating an image, classify the information as IMAGE, HYBRID, NATIVE_TEXT, SOURCE_VIEW, NOT_NEEDED or OFF_DOMAIN_HANDOFF. Precision-heavy warranty/logistics/care content may be better as native text/hybrid than as a dense poster.

## Product presence
Use REQUIRED / OPTIONAL / FORBIDDEN / NOT_APPLICABLE during planning, then resolve to PRESENT / ABSENT / N/A for production. A proof, FAQ, ingredient, documentation or atmosphere asset is not defective merely because a full product packshot is absent.

## Surface copy budget
Use semantic budgets first: primary decision answer, supporting proof and copy layers. Only use hard character/occupancy limits when a current platform rule supplies them. Overflow secondary detail to the next native surface rather than shrinking everything into one frame.

## Per-asset Design Card
Every activated asset should define:
- consumer question, purchase barrier, entry state, target belief, exit state and RACE stage;
- commercial hypothesis, incremental information, success metric and handoff;
- creative direction plus asset expression variant;
- visual narrative and decisive moment;
- subject, action, environment, props and human/casting role;
- composition grammar, spatial rhythm, camera/view, crop, product orientation and safe zone;
- key/fill/negative fill/rim, shadow, reflection, DOF and material/CMF behavior;
- primary/secondary/proof hierarchy, visible copy, locale and deterministic typography boundary;
- product presence, product fidelity, source masters, editable/frozen layers;
- surface-native behavior and local-revision scope.

## Canonical Prompt
The runtime prompt is not a strategy summary. It is the executable compression of the Design Card. It must preserve the causal chain:

`shopper decision → visible evidence relationship → composition/camera/light/material → locked copy → product truth → surface behavior → renderer/layer plan`.

Exact approved visible text should be composed deterministically when accuracy matters; do not ask image generation to repeatedly retype approved copy.

## Anti-collapse rules
- Do not default to centered product + giant headline + three generic cards.
- Do not repeat the previous asset composition merely to maintain “brand consistency”.
- Do not add splash, particles, laboratory cues, bokeh, chrome, plants or generic lifestyle props unless they solve the current decision job.
- Do not force product presence into every frame.
- Do not use one “premium” lighting recipe across different materials, people and evidence tasks.
- Do not turn every valuable fact into an image.
