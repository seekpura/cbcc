# Surface Production Contract Authority

A platform strategy is not production-ready until each active surface has an executable content form.

Each surface production contract should specify:
- entry state and primary consumer question,
- media/working-format intent,
- first-frame/first-line requirement,
- copy density and safe-zone logic,
- product fidelity and proof visibility,
- motion/demo logic where relevant,
- audio/caption logic where relevant,
- CTA handoff,
- master-asset reuse vs recompose decision,
- measurement,
- exact-policy authority source.

Exact pixels/duration/file constraints belong to the compiled/current platform policy, not to generic knowledge. Generic production guidance must never masquerade as submission policy.
