# Visual / Photography WHY Framework

Every production parameter should connect to a commercial job.

Examples:
- focal length controls perspective and packaging distortion;
- product occupancy controls mobile recognition and room for copy;
- key light geometry controls material truth and label readability;
- negative fill controls shape separation;
- CCT/WB protects product color and skin tone;
- casting and styling establish relevance only when a human context answers a real purchase question;
- props and environments clarify use, scale, mood or category context when they add information.

Do not treat plants, splashes, labs, models or props as universal requirements or universal bans.

## Parameter-level causal logic
Lens + distance control perspective. Camera height/yaw/pitch determine which product faces are available for decision-making. Aperture/focus plane determine whether identity/proof remains readable. Key/fill/negative-fill/rim determine shape, material truth and separation. CCT/WB/color management preserve product and skin color. Reflection control must balance readability with truthful material appearance.

For each selected parameter write what shopper/business problem it solves. If a parameter does not change recognition, evidence, fit, use understanding, product truth or platform adaptation, it is probably a stylistic preference rather than a strategy decision.
