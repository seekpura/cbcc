# Causal Experiment Design Authority

## Purpose
Move beyond post-hoc A/B significance toward experiment design quality that can support commerce decisions.

## Required reasoning
- Assignment unit and randomization declaration.
- Pre-period balance diagnostics when available.
- Baseline rate, MDE, alpha and target power before outcome interpretation where possible.
- Contamination, spillover and interference review.
- Instrumentation/exposure-window/concurrent-change review.
- Segment/cohort analysis is arithmetic/exploratory unless heterogeneity was planned.
- Net incremental contribution is stronger for business decisions than attributed revenue alone.

## Boundaries
- Balance does not prove randomization.
- Significance does not repair broken assignment.
- Approximate two-proportion power does not cover cluster randomization, repeated measures or sequential designs.
- Segment differences do not prove treatment-effect heterogeneity without proper analysis.
