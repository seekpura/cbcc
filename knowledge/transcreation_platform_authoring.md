# Transcreation and Platform-native Authoring

Cross-border authoring has two separate decisions:

1. **Meaning authority:** what must remain true and strategically consistent.
2. **Expression authority:** how that meaning should be phrased, sequenced and produced for a locale and surface.

Meaning authority comes from Product Truth, approved claims, Positioning, Message Hierarchy and field/asset decision jobs. Expression authority comes from local language, search behavior, platform attention patterns and CultureContext.

A strong local version can use different sentence order, hook, CTA and rhythm while preserving the same claim strength and buyer promise. Literal similarity is not a quality metric.

For Listing, author by field job. For video/creator/paid, author by beats and consumer state. For visual assets, localize visible copy and cultural context without changing product identity or inventing local stereotypes.
