# Dynamic Asset Portfolio Curation

An asset exists because it changes a purchase decision, not because a gallery slot is empty.

## Asset existence test
An asset is justified when at least one is true:
- it answers a distinct shopper question;
- it resolves a distinct objection or expectation risk;
- it communicates proof or benefit not already understood;
- it satisfies a real platform/surface job;
- it tests a meaningful hypothesis.

For every asset document consumer question, purchase barrier, belief target, reason-to-exist, incremental information, sequence/handoff, surface, production mode, success metric and WHY it cannot simply be merged with another asset.

## Reuse and merge
The same physical asset may serve multiple display surfaces when the platform actually reuses it. Do not create duplicate physical designs for the same upload placement merely because the asset appears on more than one surface.

## Strong existence rule
A professional asset portfolio records `Consumer Decision Question + Strategic Job + Surface Need + Incremental Information + Business Hypothesis + Success Metric`. Same question/job/surface should merge by default. Cross-surface duplicates require a materially different native format, shopper state, information depth or CTA.

## Production continuity
Curation changes whether an asset should exist; it does not collapse delivery. Every activated asset keeps its independent 00–11 package and production/review/revision trace.
