# Dynamic Asset Portfolio Curation

An asset exists because it changes a purchase decision, not because a gallery slot is empty. The **host Agent is the portfolio curator**. Runtime may preserve decision lineage and validate the portfolio, but it does not decide a repeated gallery recipe.

## Asset existence test
An asset is justified when at least one is true:
- it answers a distinct shopper question;
- it resolves a distinct objection or expectation risk;
- it communicates proof or benefit not already understood;
- it satisfies a real platform/surface job;
- it tests a meaningful hypothesis;
- it creates a necessary emotional, sensory, contextual or identity bridge that materially improves the decision journey.

For every asset document consumer question, purchase barrier, belief target, reason-to-exist, incremental information, sequence/handoff, surface, production mode, success metric and WHY it cannot simply be merged with another asset.

## Portfolio-level curation authority
Before authoring individual visuals, the Agent curates the portfolio as a whole. Decide dynamically:
- which shopper decisions deserve separate assets and which should merge or disappear;
- which information should be IMAGE, HYBRID, NATIVE_TEXT, SOURCE_VIEW, VIDEO, CREATOR, another native carrier, NOT_NEEDED or OFF_DOMAIN_HANDOFF;
- the **product-presence rhythm** across the portfolio: hero, supporting, detail, absent or not applicable according to each job;
- the visual-carrier rhythm: product identity, human action, evidence plane, source documentation, material study, environmental context, editorial information design, comparison, sequence, etc.;
- scale, density, focal-path and negative-space rhythm across adjacent assets;
- continuity tokens that should repeat and design decisions that should deliberately change;
- where the portfolio needs calm, proof, desire, explanation, trust, use context or conversion pressure.

A portfolio is not improved by placing the product in every frame. Repeated product presence is justified only when each occurrence performs a distinct decision job.

## Per-asset Art Direction handoff
Every activated visual asset receives its own Agent-authored Art Direction. At minimum resolve the decisions relevant to that carrier:
- visual carrier and decisive moment;
- product presence and role, with `presence_why`;
- subject / action / environment / props;
- composition / spatial rhythm / scale / focal path / negative space;
- evidence hierarchy and information relationship;
- camera, lighting, material, CMF and people only when relevant;
- source-reference role and product-fidelity policy;
- continuity tokens plus the deliberate difference from neighboring assets.

The Prompt Compiler consumes this Art Direction. It does not fill missing design decisions with a packshot, centered hero, left/right poster layout, card grid or generic category imagery.

## Product presence is independent from fidelity
A source product image can remain a Product Truth or identity reference while the product is visually absent. `TRUTH_REFERENCE_ONLY` does not create a product layer. `FORBIDDEN` means no packshot, corner product, ghost silhouette or product podium.

If product presence or required Art Direction is unresolved, return the asset to the Agent. Missing creative decisions are not permission for runtime to improvise.

## Reuse and merge
The same physical asset may serve multiple display surfaces when the platform actually reuses it. Do not create duplicate physical designs for the same upload placement merely because the asset appears on more than one surface.

## Strong existence rule
A professional asset portfolio records `Consumer Decision Question + Strategic Job + Surface Need + Incremental Information + Business Hypothesis + Success Metric`. Same question/job/surface should merge by default. Cross-surface duplicates require a materially different native format, shopper state, information depth or CTA.

## Portfolio anti-collapse review
Before production, review the portfolio as a sequence rather than as isolated prompts:
- Are too many assets using the same composition grammar?
- Is the product appearing simply because a source image exists?
- Are multiple assets doing the same visual job with different copy?
- Is one direction-level layout overriding asset-specific design?
- Are photography parameters being generated when the carrier is graphic/source/native-text instead?
- Does every product appearance, person, prop, texture, diagram or visual effect have a decision role?
- Do adjacent assets create useful rhythm and progression rather than a cloned carousel?

## Production continuity
Curation changes whether an asset should exist and how it should be designed; it does not collapse delivery. Every activated asset keeps its independent package and production/review/revision trace. Shared systems provide continuity, while per-asset Art Direction remains authoritative for the actual pixels/behavior.
