# Category Evidence Reconciliation Authority

## Purpose
Category profiles are priors, not winners by default. Current project evidence can change the order of objections, proof and message modules.

## Authority order
1. Product Truth and verified current policy/evidence.
2. Own shopper questions, feedback and observed own-product behavior.
3. Current market/search/review/competitor evidence within its declared scope.
4. Category commerce prior.
5. Generic fallback.

## Required reasoning
- WHAT changed from the category prior.
- WHY current evidence justifies changing or retaining the prior.
- EVIDENCE and source boundary.
- CONSUMER decision state affected.
- PLATFORM expression, without turning platform mechanics into Product Truth.
- BUSINESS consequence.
- EXECUTION change in Listing/Content/Asset/Offer.
- MEASUREMENT needed to validate the change.

## Boundaries
- Market evidence may reorder priorities but MUST NOT create an own-product fact, claim, compatibility, certification, service promise or sensory result.
- Competitor proof/message norms are comparison evidence only.
- Review/search volume describes the supplied sample; it is not population truth.
- Category priors remain useful when evidence is missing or weak.
