# Research Evidence Ingestion / Dedup / Version Lineage

Large research datasets need provenance before interpretation. Exact deterministic dedup is opt-in and limited to same-source exact row fingerprints. Cross-source equality is reported, never auto-deleted. Explicit entity keys with changing fingerprints are version/conflict review items. Later versions do not automatically become more truthful; Strategy Authority must retain source/version lineage.

Decision rule: ingestion quality changes confidence and sample accounting, but never manufactures market meaning.
