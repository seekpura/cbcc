# Professional Strategy Authoring Standard

The system is not complete when it merely proves that files exist or that a workflow can pass gates. The professional deliverable must explain the commercial decision.

## Decision depth
Every important recommendation should answer:
- What are we deciding?
- Why is this the best current decision?
- Which evidence supports it?
- Which shopper question or barrier does it address?
- What is specific to the platform/surface?
- What is specific to culture/localization?
- What business outcome should it affect?
- What exactly changes in execution?
- How will we know whether it worked?

## Strategy order
Product Truth → Market / Competitor / Keyword → Product & SKU / Economics → Audience / Culture → Positioning → Message Hierarchy → Listing/Search → Creative / Photography / CMF → Platform Content Matrix → Dynamic Assets → Production → Measurement / Feedback / Experiment.

## Output quality
Avoid generic phrases such as "use appropriate CTA", "target audience", "premium style", "optimize conversion" unless followed by the actual decision and reason. A strategy section should be executable by the next team without them re-inventing the logic.

## Interpretation before recommendation
Deterministic tools calculate quantities; Strategy Authority interprets what they mean and what should change. A metric without a decision implication is analysis, not strategy. A recommendation without evidence scope and an alternative explanation is overconfidence.

## Cross-domain integration
- Market evidence can change the competitive frame, price/configuration hypotheses and search-entry plan.
- Unit economics can invalidate an attractive discount, affiliate or paid-growth idea.
- Positioning determines message priority, but Listing/Search determines where each message can appear naturally.
- Culture changes language, representation, scene and interaction detail; it does not create product value.
- Photography/CMF choices must preserve the physical product while increasing decision clarity.
- Feedback and paid-media diagnosis should revise the smallest causal layer possible.

## Authority boundary
Product Truth and deterministic calculations are authoritative for facts/math. Professional prompts and knowledge authorities are authoritative for reasoning methods. Project-specific Strategy Authority records the actual recommendation and WHY. No layer silently overwrites another.
