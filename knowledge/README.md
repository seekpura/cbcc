# CBCC Commerce Knowledge Layer

This layer supplies reusable commercial reasoning to the strategy runtime. It is not a platform-rule dump and does not override current official evidence.

Domains:
- product truth and claim/value translation;
- market structure, price/configuration opportunity and competitor interpretation;
- audience, culture, race/ethnicity and intersectionality as contextual evidence rather than behavioral stereotypes;
- positioning, value proposition, message hierarchy and objection handling;
- RACE, STP/JTBD, 4P, funnel and behavior/belief models;
- listing/search conversion architecture;
- creative strategy, CMF, photography and casting;
- dynamic surface/content/asset planning;
- advertising economics, feedback diagnosis, experiment and learning.

The runtime should use knowledge to design a positive commercial answer first. Governance checks evidence and submission-sensitive claims in the background.
