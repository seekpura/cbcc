# v0.6.47 — FULL Commercial Depth & Host Materialization Recovery

## Why
v0.6.46 restored commercial lineage in Runtime, but a real ChatGPT Skills-only delivery could still bypass Runtime Export and produce a thinner Host-authored ZIP. The observed regression lost machine authorities, Chinese companions, non-gallery Asset Universe candidates and production depth while a boolean/existence audit still reported PASS.

## Fixes
1. **Host-native FULL Materialization Contract = Runtime Export Contract.** FULL delivery must materialize the same human objects, machine authorities, audits, source/media contracts and per-asset production objects regardless of execution path.
2. **FULL Depth V3.** Headings, booleans and file counts no longer prove depth. Market/region/audience/culture has explicit platform/geography/age/gender/income/language/race-ethnicity/religion/holiday/payment/logistics/regulation/platform-behavior/search/competition/price/intersectionality/taboo statuses and decision consequences.
3. **Product Truth A/B/C discipline.** Facts and claims retain selling-point/benefit/emotional-value/boundary/source. Unsupported claims stay C / pending evidence.
4. **Framework relationship contract.** RACE = Research→Action→Communication→Evaluation; STP+JTBD, AIDA/FABE and 4P/Funnel are explicit decision lenses with WHY rather than mandatory empty templates.
5. **Culture + localization operating depth.** Intersectionality, religion/taboo, language/back-translation, holidays, payment, logistics and local regulation are explicit evidence/assumption/to-verify objects.
6. **Conditional inclusive photography.** People are not mandatory. When people/hands/body are selected, deep-skin shadow protection, skin-tone exposure, bounce/diffusion, hair texture, body/disability/age representation and AI-bias review become P0 production requirements.
7. **Resource tradeoffs.** FULL projects state constraints, keep/cut decisions and WHY using P0/P1/P2; “all of it” is not a resource plan.
8. **Asset Universe export.** `ASSET_UNIVERSE.json` preserves all non-archive candidates and clearly separates total inventory from Activation Capacity. Gallery 7/9 slots cannot delete Paid/Test/Rotation/PDP/A+/Social/Creator/Video candidates.
9. **Video/materialization restore.** Active video/creator objects are materialized under `video/`; active commercial candidates must have a production object or an explicit hold/not-needed reason.
10. **Machine-authority preservation.** Top-level artifact JSON is copied into `supporting_analysis/`; stable strategy aliases and decision-lineage objects are preserved.
11. **Language parity.** Foreign human-facing Listing/Visual/Video/Ads/Review/Research copy keeps Chinese companion; English Model Prompt is audited for provider-facing language purity.
12. **Per-asset depth / anti-template regression.** Design Cards require subject/scene/props/evidence/composition/CMF/portfolio delta/measurement decisions; extreme Production Notes / Measurement repetition is flagged.
