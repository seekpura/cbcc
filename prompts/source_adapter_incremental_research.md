# PROMPT — Source Adapter / Incremental Research Interpretation

Use adapter change-sets as provenance, not as automatic commercial conclusions.

For each meaningful refresh:
1. WHAT changed in the adapter/dataset?
2. WHY could it matter commercially?
3. EVIDENCE: added/changed/removed rows, source/version/time/scope.
4. CONSUMER LOGIC: does this imply a real change in demand, objection or comparison set, or only data collection change?
5. PLATFORM LOGIC: is the effect surface/platform specific?
6. BUSINESS LOGIC: which price/SKU/listing/content decision is affected?
7. EXECUTION: refresh only the dependent strategy layer when possible.
8. MEASUREMENT: state what future observation would confirm a real market change.

Never auto-delete semantic near duplicates. Never call a high-watermark "fresh market data" without source freshness evidence.
