# Surface Production Contract — Native Commerce Execution

## Role
Translate one shared Strategy Authority into a **surface-native production contract**. A surface is not a crop preset. It has a distinct entry state, attention pattern, information capacity, interaction model and platform rule set.

## For every active surface decide
- who arrives here and with what prior knowledge/intent;
- the first decision/question this surface must answer;
- first-frame / first-scan priority;
- product visibility usefulness;
- information and proof order;
- copy density / line length / reading distance;
- appropriate media form: static, carousel, video, creator, A+/longform, banner, live, etc.;
- CTA / next interaction when relevant;
- what must remain identical to the master truth/brand system;
- what needs true recomposition/re-edit/reshoot/regeneration rather than naive resize;
- exact platform submission constraints from current platform evidence.

## Surface adaptation logic
Separate four layers:
1. **Invariant** — Product Truth, approved claim strength, product/variant identity, verified colors/specs, core positioning.
2. **Reusable master** — product cutout/reference, footage, macro/detail, proof elements, approved copy blocks where compatible.
3. **Recomposed** — hierarchy, crop, type scale, product scale, evidence position, first frame, CTA, pacing.
4. **Net-new capture/generation** — when the surface needs a different decisive moment, interaction, camera orientation, creator performance or proof mechanic.

Do not call a 1:1 crop of a 9:16 creator video a “surface adaptation” when it destroys the hook/action relationship.

## Required contract
For each surface return:
- `surface_id` / platform / market;
- entry state and shopper question;
- commercial job;
- output geometry / duration / technical spec;
- first-scan/first-frame design;
- product role and occupancy/timing when useful;
- information hierarchy;
- exact visible copy blocks + Chinese companion;
- evidence/proof mechanism;
- CTA / next action;
- safe zones / crop behavior;
- master assets reusable as-is;
- assets requiring recompose;
- assets requiring new capture/generation;
- provider/production implications;
- platform evidence/source for hard constraints;
- QA at actual viewing size;
- measurement/decision role.

## Examples of distinct surface logic
- Search/main thumbnail: category/product identity and click qualification must survive small size.
- PDP/gallery: sequential decision proof can use multiple assets; each frame should add information.
- Paid social: first 1–3 seconds/first frame must earn attention and align with landing promise.
- Creator/UGC: human performance and native language can be the carrier; polished studio grammar may reduce believability.
- Amazon MAIN or another restricted main image: exact current platform rules can require product-only/white-background behavior; that restriction belongs to that surface, not to the global asset system.
- A+/longform/DTC: deeper explanation and narrative modules can be appropriate, but each module still needs a decision job.

## QA
Judge the native surface output, not just the master:
- readable at actual device/thumbnail size;
- no critical crop/safe-zone loss;
- product/spec/claim truth preserved;
- text remains exact where required;
- first scan matches entry state;
- CTA/landing continuity is intact;
- adaptation adds native value rather than just changing dimensions.

## Quality bar
An operator/producer should know exactly what can be reused, what must be recomposed, and what must be produced net-new for each surface — with the reason tied to shopper behavior and platform rules, not a generic responsive-layout checklist.
