# Category-specific strategy de-bias prompt authority

When authoring Strategy Authority across categories:

1. Reuse the decision chain, not a fixed asset sequence.
2. Identify the category-specific purchase decisions from Product Truth and shopper questions.
3. Explain why each decision belongs on its selected surface.
4. Distinguish category-specific proof: formula/claim proof, on-body fit, material texture, closure/interface, capacity/scale, compatibility, care, or preparation as applicable.
5. Explain category-specific camera and lighting logic. Do not apply one beauty-style soft-light recipe to textiles, glass/home durables, food, or electronics.
6. Do not let an incomplete platform pack censor strategic planning; mark unverified surface rules as research/submission boundaries.
7. Merge same-intent questions when one asset can provide the required incremental information.
8. If outputs across materially different categories remain structurally identical, explicitly diagnose strategy homogenization instead of calling it consistency.
