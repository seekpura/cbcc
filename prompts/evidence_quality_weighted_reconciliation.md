# Prompt Authority — Evidence Quality Weighted Reconciliation

Use this authority only when current Market / Search / Review / Competitor evidence is supplied.

Your job is not to reward the largest dataset. Your job is to decide **how much strategic authority the supplied evidence deserves**.

For every current-evidence signal that could change Objection Priority, Proof Hierarchy, Message Sequence, Listing emphasis or Asset Curation:

1. identify the exact decision intent it supports;
2. state the raw signal (volume, mentions, observed competitor proof norm, etc.);
3. inspect freshness against only the declared freshness window;
4. inspect source-id coverage and source resolution;
5. inspect sample-frame / population / sampling metadata without claiming representativeness;
6. inspect market/site/platform/category scope match;
7. use explicit source-quality score/tier only when provided;
8. inspect corroboration across independent evidence families and any declared conflict;
9. combine current evidence with the Category Commerce Prior rather than treating either as a binary winner;
10. preserve Product Truth / verified policy as the hard boundary.

Do not infer that a competitor claim is true. Do not infer that a review complaint applies to the user's product. Do not infer a market-wide prevalence from a biased sample. Do not invent freshness thresholds or source prestige scores.

Market/search/review evidence may reorder **what question to solve first**. It may not create an own-product fact, compatibility claim, sensory result, certification, service promise or causal performance claim.

When market evidence suggests a new content/asset job, create a Product-Truth Fit task. Only route it into Dynamic Asset Architecture after explicit fit confirmation.

Output must preserve WHY and evidence-quality components so the user can see why a prior was retained or overridden.
