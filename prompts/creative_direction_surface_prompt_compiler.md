# Creative Direction / Surface / Canonical Prompt Compiler

## Objective
Turn Product Truth + Product Visual Intelligence + shopper decision + selected Creative Direction + Surface role + locale + Photography/CMF into one production-ready asset contract and bilingual canonical prompt.

## Required reasoning order
1. Compile or consume the 6–12 mechanism **Creative Opportunity Universe** for this SKU/target; do not pick from a fixed style menu.
2. Synthesize/select 3–5 materially different directions from those mechanisms, then lock the current direction.
3. Identify the exact unresolved shopper question and current → target belief.
4. Decide which RACE stage the asset serves: Research / Action / Communication / Evaluation.
5. Decide the best information representation: IMAGE / HYBRID / NATIVE_TEXT / SOURCE_VIEW / NOT_NEEDED / OFF_DOMAIN_HANDOFF.
6. Resolve product presence and fidelity.
7. Select an asset-level visual expression that remains coherent with the selected direction but materially differs from adjacent assets when decision/evidence differs.
8. Define the decisive moment and subject/action/environment/props.
9. Define composition, camera/view, crop/orientation, lighting, material/CMF and human/casting only as required by the decision job.
10. Resolve primary/secondary/proof hierarchy and surface copy budget.
11. Bind exact approved visible copy and source product masters.
12. Compile CN + EN prompts, machine visual contract, renderer recipe, local-revision layers and QA targets.

## Mandatory output qualities
- Positive-first description of the desired commercial image/video.
- Product identity and pack text never invented.
- Copy is visually attributable to evidence in frame.
- Camera and lighting explain why they make the decision-critical evidence readable.
- Human models appear because fit/use/relevance requires them, not because “lifestyle” was selected.
- Adjacent assets have a visible rhythm delta when their decision jobs differ.
- Surface behavior is native; no mechanical resize of another surface.
- The final prompt contains production instructions, not internal strategy-state/gate chatter.

## Paid / Creator / Live
Paid creative must have its own objective, audience state, tension, proof/offer, landing handoff, at least two materially different creative concepts and test hypothesis. Creator content is first-person/action native with real handling; Live is an interactive objection/demo/offer loop, not a long linear ad.
