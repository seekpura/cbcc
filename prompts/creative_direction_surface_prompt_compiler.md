# Creative Direction / Surface / Canonical Prompt Compiler

## Objective
Compile a production-ready asset contract and bilingual Canonical Prompt from **already-authored commerce strategy + portfolio curation + per-asset Art Direction**.

The Prompt Compiler is a compiler, not the creative director. It must preserve selected creative decisions and renderer-relevant production parameters. It must not invent the asset’s core composition, product role, product presence, subject, scene, hierarchy or visual carrier merely because those decisions are missing upstream.

## Authority order
`Product Truth / Evidence → Commerce Strategy → Portfolio Curation → Creative Direction → Per-asset Art Direction → Surface adaptation → Canonical Prompt → Renderer`

Later layers may faithfully translate or technically adapt earlier decisions. They may not silently redesign them.

## Required reasoning / consumption order
1. Consume the current-product Creative Opportunity exploration and the selected Creative Direction. Exploration depth and direction count are task-dependent; do not use a fixed style menu or numeric quota.
2. Consume the exact unresolved shopper question, purchase barrier and current → target belief for this asset.
3. Consume the chosen information representation / visual carrier for this asset: IMAGE / HYBRID / NATIVE_TEXT / SOURCE_VIEW / VIDEO / CREATOR / NOT_NEEDED / OFF_DOMAIN_HANDOFF or another explicitly authored carrier.
4. Consume the **explicit Product Presence Decision**: `HERO_REQUIRED | REQUIRED | SUPPORTING | OPTIONAL | DETAIL_ONLY | FORBIDDEN`, plus product role, scale relationship, identity requirement and source-reference role.
5. Consume the per-asset decisive moment and subject/action/environment/props.
6. Consume the per-asset composition grammar, spatial rhythm, hierarchy, evidence plane, camera/view/crop and negative-space strategy.
7. Consume the asset-level lighting / CMF / material / color / human/casting decisions only when relevant to the chosen medium and decision job.
8. Consume the primary/secondary/proof hierarchy, copy budget, typography role and Surface-native behavior.
9. Bind approved copy, Product Truth, evidence, source references and fidelity boundaries.
10. Compile CN + EN prompts, machine visual contract, renderer recipe, local-revision layers, parameter trace and QA targets.

## Product presence rules
- **No silent default to product subject.** Missing `product_presence` is an upstream Art Direction gap, not permission to insert the product.
- `FORBIDDEN` means downstream render instructions and layer plans must not add the product as subject, packshot, signature, corner pack, ghosted silhouette, podium object or composited product layer.
- When `source_reference_role = TRUTH_REFERENCE_ONLY`, source product files may constrain facts / identity / claim honesty but must not become visible subjects unless the Art Direction separately requests them.
- `OPTIONAL` means product visibility is genuinely optional and must be resolved by the authored design; it is not equivalent to REQUIRED.
- Product identity/fidelity metadata can remain in the machine contract even when the product is absent; factual authority is not the same thing as visual presence.

## Layout / composition rules
- Never default to `text-left / product-right`, `product-left / text-right`, centered product + headline, card grid, podium hero, split-screen or another reusable poster skeleton solely because the asset is an ecommerce image.
- A Surface safe zone constrains placement; it does not author the layout.
- Copy budget constrains information density; it does not determine left/right geometry.
- Brand coherence comes from the selected design system and appropriate continuity tokens. Adjacent assets may share color/type/material/scene logic while using materially different spatial structures when their jobs differ.
- Do not pursue arbitrary variety. Reuse is valid when the same visual system genuinely answers the same decision job; visible differences must come from different information relationships, subjects, evidence, actions, scale or Surface behavior.

## Mandatory output qualities
- Positive-first description of the desired commercial image/video/design.
- The prompt reflects the **Asset Art Direction authority**, not a generic ecommerce template.
- Product identity and pack text are never invented.
- Copy is visually attributable to evidence in frame where evidence is required.
- Camera and lighting appear only when relevant and explain how they make decision-critical evidence readable.
- Human models appear because fit/use/relevance/representation requires them, not because “lifestyle” was selected.
- Product absence is respected when the authored design makes context, proof, material, information, source/document or human/action content primary.
- Adjacent assets have the intended rhythm delta when their decision jobs differ.
- Surface behavior is native; no mechanical resize of another surface.
- The final render prompt contains production instructions, not internal strategy-state/gate chatter, IDs, RACE/KPI metadata or audit prose.

## Compiler failure behavior
If a requested production Prompt reaches the compiler without an explicit per-asset Art Direction decision that materially affects pixels—especially product presence, visual carrier, composition hierarchy or subject—the compiler must expose the missing design decision in the machine / human production contract. It must **not** hide the gap by falling back to a product hero or generic left/right layout.

This is a production-readiness issue, not permission to erase the strategy/Listing/curation work already completed.

## Paid / Creator / Live
Paid creative must have its own objective, audience state, tension, proof/offer, landing handoff, materially different concepts when useful and a test hypothesis. Creator content is first-person/action native with real handling; Live is an interactive objection/demo/offer loop, not a long linear ad. None of these surfaces inherits PDP layout merely because the same product is being sold.
