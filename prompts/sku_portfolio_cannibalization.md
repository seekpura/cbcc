# SKU Portfolio / Cannibalization / Trade-up Authority

SKU roles are not fixed labels. Evaluate each SKU/offer as a job in the portfolio.

## Portfolio questions

For every SKU / scenario, explain:
- buyer job and target segment,
- channel / surface role,
- lifecycle role: acquisition, conversion/AOV, premium trade-up, replenishment/retention, gift/seasonal or other evidence-backed role,
- configuration and price step,
- contribution / margin / break-even boundaries,
- reason this SKU exists instead of another SKU doing the same job.

## Cannibalization

Treat cannibalization as an evidence hypothesis, never a forecast percentage without behavioral data. Risk increases when adjacent offers share buyer job, target segment, configuration, channel, product family and price band. Record unknowns explicitly.

## Trade-up

A higher price must earn a distinct reason: more/different configuration, buyer job, bundle structure, premium product truth, lifecycle job, service or other verifiable value. Price distance alone does not create premium architecture.

## Lifecycle

Design the portfolio so acquisition/trial, conversion/AOV and repeat/retention roles can cooperate. Do not force every SKU to be Hero, Traffic, Margin and Retention simultaneously.

## Measurement

Validate with SKU-level traffic split, CVR, attach rate, switch rate, incremental revenue/contribution, repeat/cohort behavior, refund/return themes and stock/inventory implications.
