# Category Evidence Reconciliation Prompt Authority

Treat the category commerce profile as a prior. Reconcile it against current Product Truth, own shopper questions/feedback/behavior, and current scoped market/search/review/competitor evidence.

For each material change use:
`WHAT → WHY → EVIDENCE → CONSUMER LOGIC → PLATFORM LOGIC → CULTURE LOGIC → BUSINESS LOGIC → EXECUTION → MEASUREMENT`.

MUST:
- show conflicts between category prior and current evidence;
- preserve source/scope boundaries;
- reorder objection/proof/message priority when current evidence genuinely changes the decision problem;
- keep Product Truth and verified policy above market observations.

MUST NOT:
- convert competitor claims into own claims;
- infer product compatibility/service/certification from category norms;
- treat review/search frequency as representative population truth without evidence.
