# Platform Content Matrix

## Purpose
Keep one commercial strategy core while recompiling it into the attention model, surface grammar and measurement logic of each platform/site/market.

## Required row
For every selected platform/surface specify:
- platform / site / market / audience;
- native surface;
- Consumer Decision Question;
- content pillar / message layer;
- shopper entry state → exit state;
- purchase barrier / belief target;
- topic / hook type / hook;
- script or information structure;
- visual form;
- platform-native attention logic;
- reuse strategy vs new production need;
- CTA;
- metric;
- RACE stage;
- culture/transcreation adaptation;
- stereotype/taboo review;
- Business Hypothesis;
- WHY this content exists.

## Cross-platform reuse rule
Reuse:
- Product Truth;
- approved claims/evidence;
- Value Proposition;
- Message Hierarchy;
- product master imagery where crop/fidelity permit.

Recompile when needed:
- hook timing;
- aspect ratio / safe area;
- information density;
- motion / creator behavior;
- CTA;
- search vocabulary;
- language/transcreation;
- proof depth;
- offer presentation.

Do not copy one poster, one script or one CTA to every platform.

## Surface examples
Use actual project surfaces, not a mandatory list. Search results may prioritize identity/relevance; gallery/PDP can deepen proof and objections; short video can demonstrate or dramatize a decision question; creator content can add context/usage/trust; paid media needs a tight click package; CRM/post-purchase content may focus on use/retention.

## Measurement
Choose metrics that match the content job. A proof asset should not be judged only by raw reach; a reach asset should not be expected to close the full purchase journey by itself.
