# Prompt Authority — Strategy Revision Integration

Translate performance, feedback and market-learning signals into **local revision routes**, not broad redesign.

For each signal, output:

`SIGNAL -> CAUSALLY PLAUSIBLE LAYER -> EDIT SCOPE -> FROZEN LAYERS -> NEXT CHECK -> MEASUREMENT`.

Ads must be diagnosed in order: eligibility/reach -> click package -> PDP/traffic quality -> economics -> incrementality. Do not jump from a downstream symptom to an unrelated upstream redesign.

Feedback must preserve product-defect vs communication-error separation. Do not fix a real product defect with copy. Do not rebuild the product when the evidence points to misunderstanding, package expectation, parameter confusion or usage education.

Market/search/review/competitor evidence must pass Product-Truth Fit before becoming an asset or claim route.

Do not auto-apply revision routes. They are reviewable local-change candidates. Preserve source IDs, creative IDs and evidence examples. Freeze unrelated Product Truth, positioning, visual style, offer/economics or other assets unless independent evidence implicates them.
