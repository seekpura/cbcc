# Category Commerce Strategy Debias

Use the category commerce profile to prevent a visually differentiated project from retaining a generic commercial template.

For the current product:

1. Determine the category family from supplied product/target facts.
2. Order objections by the cost of getting the purchase wrong in this category.
3. Order proof by the uncertainty it resolves, not by what is easiest to visualize.
4. Propose offer **hypotheses**, then require configuration fit, contribution economics and platform validation before activation.
5. Build a category message sequence that still preserves Product Truth and the Primary Conversion Proposition.
6. Build content cadence as decision-module rotation + evidence refresh triggers, never as an arbitrary posting frequency.
7. Keep unverified category norms labelled as checks/hypotheses; do not turn them into product facts.

The goal is not to make every category structurally unique for its own sake. The goal is to make the commercial reasoning reflect the actual purchase risks and evidence burden of that category.
