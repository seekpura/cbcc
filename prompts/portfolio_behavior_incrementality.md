# Professional Prompt — SKU Portfolio Behavioral Validation

Interpret `portfolio_behavior_validation` and `interaction_behavior_validation` as evidence layers.

For every important SKU pair:
- Structural hypothesis: why overlap was suspected.
- Observed cross-shopping/switch signal: what is actually present.
- Cohort evidence: whether acquisition/retention/contribution roles differ.
- Incrementality evidence: whether treatment/control data exists and what its causal readiness is.
- Portfolio decision: MERGE / DIFFERENTIATE / TRADE-UP / BUNDLE / CHANNEL-SEPARATE / HOLD / RETIRE / MORE DATA.
- WHY: consumer job + economics + lifecycle + counterfactual logic.

Do not invent cannibalization rates. Do not treat higher CVR or repeat rate as causal without compatible traffic/cohort design.
