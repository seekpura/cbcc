# Product / SKU / Offer Strategy

## Purpose
Translate Product Truth + market/customer evidence + unit economics into a portfolio where every SKU and offer has a buyer job and a business job. Roles are dynamic, not a mandatory template.

## Candidate roles
Traffic, Hero, Margin, Premium, Bundle, Trial, Retention/Replenishment, Gift, Seasonal, Channel Exclusive and other project-specific roles are allowed. No role is required and one SKU may change role by channel or campaign.

## Required SKU decision card
For every SKU/scenario specify:
- **WHAT** role it plays and confidence level.
- **WHO / JTBD** it serves and the decision barrier it removes.
- **WHY** this SKU deserves a distinct role instead of being a duplicate variant.
- **EVIDENCE** from demand, market/configuration, contribution, lifecycle or channel task.
- **PRICE / CONFIGURATION LOGIC** and relationship to adjacent SKUs.
- **CHANNEL JOB**: search acquisition, creator conversion, bundle AOV, retention, etc.
- **CANNIBALIZATION / CONFUSION RISK**.
- **EXECUTION** in listing, offer and content.
- **MEASUREMENT** and what would cause the role to change.

## Price ladder
A price ladder is valid only when each step has a distinct value reason.
- Explain the product/configuration/value change between steps.
- Calculate absolute and percentage price gaps.
- Check contribution at each step, not only gross margin percentage.
- Premium requires a stronger value/proof signal; a higher price alone does not create premium positioning.
- Trial requires low-friction entry without creating a permanently unprofitable acquisition unit.

## Shared growth budget
Coupon, affiliate commission and paid-media spend consume the same contribution pool.
- Start from price minus product/fulfillment/platform/payment/tax/refund costs.
- Define minimum target contribution before allocating growth spend.
- Calculate coupon headroom, affiliate ceiling, allowable CPA and break-even ROAS from the same base.
- Never present each percentage as independently affordable.

## Offer design
For each offer answer:
- What purchase behavior should it change: first purchase, AOV, trial, replenishment, creator conversion, inventory clearing, giftability?
- Why does the user perceive genuine value rather than artificial discounting?
- Which SKU/configuration makes the offer operationally and economically coherent?
- What is the floor contribution and stopping rule?

## Scenario analysis
Run at least the scenarios supplied by the project and sensitivity around material variables when available:
- discount;
- affiliate rate;
- ad cost / CPA;
- refund allowance;
- fulfillment or landed cost;
- bundle composition.

Use scenarios to expose trade-offs, not to produce false precision. Missing costs remain explicit unknowns.

## Integration
The final SKU/Offer strategy must feed Positioning, Listing, Platform Content, Dynamic Assets and Ads diagnosis. If a role does not change any downstream execution or metric, reconsider whether the role is meaningful.
