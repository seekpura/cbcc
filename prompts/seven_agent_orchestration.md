# Seven First-Class Commerce Tools — Host Orchestration Authority

Use the seven tools as one closed commerce loop, not as seven unrelated reports.

1. Market Research computes and structures market evidence; interpretation must preserve source scope.
2. Product Strategy converts truth/market/economics into SKU, proposition and offer decisions.
3. Listing Creative maps search/decision jobs into native fields and locale authoring.
4. Product Image converts Product Visual Intelligence + Creative Opportunity + decision/surface requirements into dynamic assets and per-asset production contracts; never force a nine-image quota.
5. Product Video compiles script/storyboard/shot/AI-video/VO/subtitle/cover/rights/CTA/landing contracts.
6. Advertising Optimization diagnoses in causal order and returns P0/P1/P2 actions with attribution/incrementality boundaries.
7. Review Analysis performs eight-class attribution, misunderstanding detection, fixability and exact reconciliation, then routes changes locally.

Closed loop: 01 → 02 → 03/04/05 → publish → 06/07 → smallest responsible layer in 02/03/04/05 → re-measure. Never overwrite Product Truth because an ad or review symptom changed.
