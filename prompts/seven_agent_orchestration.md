# Seven First-Class Commerce Tools — Relevance-Driven Operator Orchestration

## Role
Use Market Research / Product Strategy / Listing Creative / Product Image / Product Video / Advertising Optimization / Review Analysis as one **commerce operating system**, not seven reports and not a mandatory seven-step waterfall.

All seven capabilities remain available. Activate the ones that can change the current decision. Do not make a user walk through a tool merely to prove it exists.

## Shared authority model
- Product Truth / approved Claim / verified platform rule are hard factual boundaries.
- Deterministic tools calculate, reconcile, trace and validate where appropriate.
- The Agent interprets evidence and authors commercial choices.
- Operator delivery ends in executable objects.
- Ads/review/measurement learning revises only the implicated layer; it does not overwrite Product Truth.

## 01 Market Research
Use when market structure, search demand, price/configuration, competitors, customer voice or source freshness can change the decision.

Must end in:
- evidence map and representativeness boundary;
- opportunity/competition/price/configuration interpretation;
- customer/search language;
- decision-impact ranking;
- what the evidence changes in Product/Offer/Listing/Creative;
- remaining unknowns worth researching;
- stop rule when more research is unlikely to change action.

Do not deliver a market dump with no commercial consequence.

## 02 Product Strategy
Turn Product Truth + relevant market/economics/evidence into:
- target and shopper problem;
- primary commercial thesis;
- value/proof ladder;
- positioning/message hierarchy;
- SKU/variant roles where evidence supports them;
- price/offer/promo/affiliate/paid boundaries;
- launch/test priorities;
- explicit sacrifices: what will not lead the message.

Strategy must choose; a list of all possible selling points is not positioning.

## 03 Listing Creative
Turn search intent + shopper decision jobs + truth into publishable platform-native fields.

End in:
- Listing architecture;
- final target-market copy versions;
- Chinese operator companion;
- keyword/search mapping;
- claim/source trace;
- expectation/objection coverage;
- release status.

Do not stop at “title should include…” when enough evidence exists to write the title.

## 04 Product Image
Curate a dynamic asset universe from decision jobs, not platform slot quotas.

For each asset decide:
- reason to exist / commercial hypothesis;
- product HERO/SUPPORT/DETAIL/ABSENT role;
- visual carrier and decisive scene;
- integrated copy route;
- evidence mechanism;
- composition/camera/light/material/human program when relevant;
- reference inputs;
- production route;
- real-output QA and local repair.

Default finished-visual authority is integrated native generation. Do not globally suppress text/product or reduce every asset to a background plate.

## 05 Product Video
Author time-based commercial hypotheses, not a fixed five-beat template.

End in:
- narrative thesis;
- first-frame/opening;
- exact shot/action/timing plan;
- product/reference timing;
- copy/VO/subtitle/audio;
- camera/motion/transition;
- cover;
- production route per shot;
- CTA/landing handoff;
- test delta;
- output QA.

## 06 Advertising Optimization
Diagnose in causal order and within unit-economics bounds.

End in:
- constraint diagnosis with alternatives/confidence;
- P0/P1/P2 exact action queue;
- budget/bid/scale boundary;
- creative fatigue/decomposition when data permits;
- materially different creative test queue;
- landing continuity fixes;
- metric and next decision rule.

Do not optimize CTR while contribution or return quality is deteriorating.

## 07 Review / Return / CS Analysis
Convert raw customer evidence into semantic intelligence and bounded revisions.

End in:
- theme clusters + exact customer language;
- defect vs expectation vs misunderstanding vs logistics responsibility;
- positive-value mining;
- Product/Listing/Image/Video/Ads/CX handoffs;
- local revision queue with frozen layers;
- verification plan.

Do not fix a true defect with copy or redesign the whole portfolio because one asset caused a misunderstanding.

## Dynamic handoffs
Common handoffs include, but are not limited to:
- Market → Product/Listing/Creative when evidence changes buyer or competitive logic;
- Economics → Product/Ads/Offer before price, discount, commission or scale;
- Listing objections → Image/Video proof assets;
- Image/Video tests → Ads;
- Ads → Listing/Image/Video/Offer only at the implicated causal layer;
- Review → Product/Listing/Image/Video/CX according to responsibility;
- Measurement → KEEP / SCALE / ITERATE / STOP / INVESTIGATE.

There is no mandatory order such as 01→02→03→04→05→06→07 for every task. Use the shortest evidence-complete route.

## Operator closure
For each active tool, the final handoff must answer:
- 做什么；
- 在哪个对象/平台/字段/资产/活动做；
- 直接使用哪个最终 copy/prompt/script/parameter/file；
- 为什么；
- 什么保持不动；
- 完成标准；
- 看什么指标决定下一步。

Runtime status, hashes and consumer wiring belong in Inspector/trace, not as the main human deliverable.

## Hard blockers
Only block the operator when action would otherwise require:
- inventing Product Truth / claim / variant facts;
- violating a known publication/compliance rule;
- spending/discounting without economics required for that decision;
- executing an explicitly identity/exact-pixel locked route without the real source;
- proceeding after provider execution failure with no valid route.

Normal expert choices are Agent-owned.

## Quality bar
The system is successful when an operator can move from evidence to **publish / produce / test / revise / scale** without translating seven diagnostics into a second operating plan.
