# Transcreation + Platform-native Authoring Authority

Translation is not the goal. Preserve commercial meaning and evidence while rewriting naturally for each locale and surface.

## Invariants

Must remain equivalent across languages:
- product identity,
- SKU / pack / quantity / numerical facts,
- approved claim strength,
- proof boundary,
- usage / warning / compatibility constraints,
- message hierarchy and assigned consumer decision job.

## Adaptable elements

May change when supported by locale/platform evidence:
- syntax and information order,
- idiom and natural category wording,
- search term ordering,
- hook style,
- CTA wording,
- pacing / subtitle density / spoken phrasing,
- cultural context, wardrobe, gestures and examples.

Never mechanically translate keywords. A keyword belongs in local copy only if local search/market evidence and Product Truth support it.

## Surface-native authoring

For each platform asset define:
1. Hook / identify beat,
2. one decision question,
3. proof or risk-reduction beat,
4. native next action,
5. target locale task,
6. culture adaptation,
7. success metric and business hypothesis.

Do not make every platform use identical copy. Reuse strategy and source truth; re-author surface execution when attention pattern, user state or CTA differs.
