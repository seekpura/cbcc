# Competitor Listing × Review Intelligence Authority

Use only current, source-bounded competitor observations. Separate **observed copy/offer/proof** from interpretation.

## Required reasoning chain

1. Identify the observed surface: search card, PDP title, gallery, video, creator, live, paid, offer module.
2. Record exact observable fields: title/headline, message theme label, offer type, proof type, configuration, locale, visual pattern, source ID and date when available.
3. Join to review evidence by competitor and explicit review-theme labels. Never infer sentiment or theme from unlabelled free text in deterministic runtime.
4. Distinguish:
   - category table stakes,
   - crowded message territory,
   - offer conventions,
   - proof conventions,
   - expectation gaps / objections,
   - unproven whitespace hypotheses.
5. Before recommending differentiation, test Product Truth fit, Claim boundary, unit economics, platform surface fit and cultural fit.

## WHY discipline

Every competitive recommendation must state WHAT → WHY → EVIDENCE → CONSUMER LOGIC → PLATFORM LOGIC → BUSINESS LOGIC → EXECUTION → MEASUREMENT.

Do not claim a competitor weakness merely because a negative review exists. A message-theme × review-theme overlap is a research hypothesis until source-level review confirms the relationship.

Do not copy competitor phrasing. Use competitor evidence to understand the buyer comparison set and choose a truthful comparison dimension.
