# Measurement / Causal Iteration

Map metrics to the layer they can diagnose:
- eligibility / traffic;
- click package;
- PDP conversion;
- economics;
- returns / feedback;
- retention.

Design A/B tests with one causal variable or the smallest possible variable set. Define control, variant, hypothesis, metric, hold-constant fields and decision rule. Route feedback to product defect vs communication misunderstanding before changing the product or redesigning everything.
