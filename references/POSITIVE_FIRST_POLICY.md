# Positive-First Creative Instruction Policy

## Principle
Start with what the asset should communicate and how it should look, feel and behave. Boundaries come second.

Preferred order:
1. asset job / consumer question;
2. product truth and approved claim;
3. desired composition, camera, lighting, CMF, human/culture behavior;
4. approved semantic elements and production method;
5. only the minimum boundaries needed to prevent factual drift, misleading implication, platform/regulatory conflict or scope drift.

## Visual elements
Plants, water, laboratory language, models, props, animals, nature, texture, motion, still-life and scientific graphics are available creative vocabulary.
They are selected when they strengthen the asset's strategic job and remain truthful. They are not a generic checklist and not a generic blacklist.

## Generation guidance
Use a positive `generation_guidance` file. If a model benefits from exclusions, put them in a short `precision_boundaries` section.
Avoid long generic negative-prompt libraries that overwhelm the intended visual direction.

## P0 boundaries
Explicit blocking language is appropriate for fabricated product facts/claims/certifications, misleading product identity, current-rule fabrication,
unresolved truth conflicts, deceptive before/after, or changes outside frozen/local-revision scope.
