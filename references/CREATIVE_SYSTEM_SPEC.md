# Creative System Specification

## Pipeline
Product Truth → Research → Curation → Creative Territories → Creative Anchor → CMF → Photography/CG → Information Design → Surface Plan → Dynamic Assets → Prompt/Production Contract → Production → QA → Revision.

## Curation
References are classified as:
- truth reference: factual/source material;
- visual reference: composition, light, CMF, styling or information-design inspiration;
- category reference: common convention/cliché to understand;
- cultural reference: evidence-backed local expression/context;
- anti-pattern reference: a pattern to differentiate from, not a permanent blacklist.

Record curation source and what is being borrowed conceptually. Never convert a visual reference into a factual product claim.

## Foundation / Creative Anchor Asset
A project may define a foundation or creative-anchor asset that establishes the strongest approved visual and information logic.
It guides the campaign system without forcing every later asset to copy its composition.

## CMF
Define Color / Material / Finish with product truth separated from campaign palette. Include surface response, gloss/matte, transparency,
metallic/specular behavior, texture scale, edge quality and interaction with light.

## Information design
Define hierarchy, reading path, mobile legibility, typography-safe zones, icon/data treatment, density and whitespace.

## Dynamic asset architecture
Asset existence is justified by consumer question, purchase barrier, new information/evidence, surface role and handoff to the next belief/action.
