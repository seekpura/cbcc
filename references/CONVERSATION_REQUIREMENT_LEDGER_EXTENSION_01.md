# CBCC Conversation Requirement Ledger Extension 01

## Source
Verbatim authority: `references/USER_LONG_REQUIREMENTS_STRATEGY_CULTURE_EXECUTION_CN.md`.

This extension exists because the cumulative ledger must preserve not only high-level concepts but also the user's exact execution locks. The source file is authoritative at the more specific level. These IDs are release/audit handles.

### REQ-051 — Verbatim long-requirement sources are mandatory authorities
When the user provides a long-form requirements block, preserve the exact requirements in a named source authority and map it into the cumulative ledger. A summarized requirement ID is not permission to discard concrete columns, sequence, thresholds, output objects or execution details from the source.

### REQ-052 — Information audit → clarification list → assumptions → continue
FULL execution follows: information audit → pending/clarification list → explicit assumptions → continue output. If information is insufficient, list at most 10 questions, then continue under stated assumptions rather than stopping. Facts, assumptions and decision rationale remain separately visible in the Assumption & Decision Log.

### REQ-053 — Product-fact four-way audit + A/B/C evidence is a required object
Before strategy, produce a Product Truth audit that explicitly separates: `真实卖点 / 可感知利益 / 情绪价值 / 不可宣称项`. Grade evidence: A = report/official certification/reproducible test; B = supply-chain/spec/formula/mechanism bounded support; C = subjective/pending. C-level material cannot become a hard claim. Claim/evidence traceability remains inspectable.

### REQ-054 — Every strategy decision requires platform × region × audience × ethnicity/culture WHY
Strategy rows cannot be generic. Each major decision must show its basis, why it fits the named platform, region, audience and race/ethnicity/cultural context, what was traded off, the executable action and the measurement signal. Cultural judgment must distinguish evidence from assumption.

### REQ-055 — Compliance/claim evidence is P0 and regulated domains are separately checked
Efficacy, ingredients, certification, price, sales volume and other factual claims are P0. Strongly regulated categories such as medical/beauty/food/financial require category-specific compliance/claim checks. Unsupported facts remain unknown/to-verify; never invent them.

### REQ-056 — Intersectionality + culture execution must be operationalized
Inspect `族裔 × 性别 × 年龄 × 收入 × 地域`, plus language/religion/festival/taboo/family structure/skin/hair/body representation when relevant. Produce a distinct `族裔文化适配检查表`. The platform content matrix must include `RACE阶段` and `族裔文化适配/禁忌校验` and use them to review script, casting, wardrobe/grooming, color and CTA. Avoid single-ethnicity labeling and stereotypes.

### REQ-057 — Inclusive visual photography is P0 with concrete execution
When people are used, Art Direction must cover deep-skin exposure, highlight/shadow protection, reflector/bounce/diffusion, hair texture/styling, body type, disability, age and AI-bias risk. The visual execution object must provide the actual relevant solution, not only a generic inclusion principle. Deep-skin photographic routes require explicit exposure/reflector/soft-light handling when relevant.

### REQ-058 — Platform execution + regional localization are complete operational objects
Platform execution considers dimensions/aspect, duration, cover/first frame, subtitles, music rights, traffic mechanism, CTA, landing page, UTM and attribution when relevant. Regional localization considers language/back-translation, festival calendar, payments/logistics, religious taboos and local law. Unverified platform rules/algorithms/regulations are marked `待查证 / TO_VERIFY`, not invented.

### REQ-059 — Resource tradeoff and P0/P1/P2 are mandatory decisions
When budget/time/capacity are limited, explicitly state what is kept and what is cut. `P0 = 不做会翻车`, `P1 = 影响效果`, `P2 = 加分项`. “全部都要” is not a tradeoff. Each strategic recommendation should show `依据 + 取舍 + 执行 + 指标`, preferably in tables where that improves operator use.

### REQ-060 — Required FULL output objects remain present without becoming a fixed UI shell
FULL completeness must expose the following semantic objects, without requiring fixed nine pages or permanent navigation:
1. 信息完整度、待确认清单、假设与决策日志；
2. 产品真实事实审计与卖点分级表（真实卖点/可感知利益/情绪价值/不可宣称项/证据等级）；
3. 平台×区域×人群×族裔文化洞察（交叉性、文化适配、禁忌、刻板印象规避）；
4. 营销框架与核心策略（定位、价值主张、信息层级、传播节奏、4P/内容漏斗）；
5. 视觉总策略与拍摄执行清单（风格、构图、HEX/对比、灯光摄影、焦段、景别、机位、场景、道具、模特服化道、平台尺寸、Shot List）；
6. 分平台内容矩阵（平台/人群/选题/钩子/脚本结构/视觉形式/CTA/指标/RACE阶段/族裔文化适配与禁忌校验）；
7. RACE传播框架执行表（阶段/目标/关键动作/输出物/平台/文化校验/指标）；
8. 族裔文化适配检查表（适配点/包容性表征/刻板印象与禁忌/校验结果/修改动作）；
9. 合规、文化敏感与风险；评估指标/A-B测试；P0/P1/P2优先级；下一步需补信息。

The nine objects are a semantic completeness contract, not a fixed page template, fixed section order or fixed menu.
