# Race / Ethnicity & Intersectionality

Required analysis dimensions when material: race/ethnicity, language, religion, festivals, taboos, skin tone, hair texture/style, body type, age, disability, family structure, clothing, gesture/body-contact norms and CTA/local language implications.

Intersectionality = ethnicity × gender × age × income × geography × lifestyle.
Do not infer fixed consumer behavior from identity. Every cultural judgment is VERIFIED, LIKELY, ASSUMPTION or PENDING with source/confidence.
