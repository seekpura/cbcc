# CBCC ASSET UNIVERSE OPEN-WORLD CONTRACT — v0.6.55

## 0. Core correction
FULL commerce work must not optimize for the smallest or fewest asset set. Build a complete commercially justified **Asset Opportunity Universe** first, then select smaller Activation Sets.

There is no fixed minimum or maximum total asset count. Platform gallery/video capacity applies only to the simultaneous Live/Activation Set and must never leak backward into candidate generation.

## 1. Three separate objects
1. **Asset Opportunity Universe** — every commercially useful candidate discovered from current product, shopper decisions, surfaces, evidence, audience/culture, offer/CTA, media form and creative/test hypotheses.
2. **Production Portfolio** — candidates selected for full Design Card + Model Prompt CN/EN + Visible Copy + Production Notes. FULL projects include production-ready Test/Rotation/Paid/A+/PDP/Social/Creator/Video candidates here, not only Live assets.
3. **Activation Set(s)** — current platform-constrained subset that goes live simultaneously.

These objects may have different counts. A 7/9/10-slot platform does not imply a 7/9/10-candidate universe.

## 2. Opportunity sweep before pruning
For each relevant project, inspect 0..N opportunities across: Gallery/Hero/Search/PDP; Benefit/Proof/Ingredient/Material/Detail; Scenario/Lifestyle/Context/Human-use; How-to/Routine/Comparison/Objection; A+/Rich Content/PDP longform; Paid Ads; Social/Seeding; Creator/UGC/Affiliate; Video/PDP Video/Short Video/LIVE; Store/Banner/Landing/Campaign/Seasonal; CRM/Lifecycle/Post-purchase; Test/Rotation/A-B/multivariate.

Relevant family absent => explicit `NOT_NEEDED / TO_VERIFY / MISSING_INPUT` + WHY. Silent omission is not sufficient coverage.

## 3. Candidate growth logic
Candidates emerge from meaningful intersections such as:
`Surface × Shopper Decision × Creative Hypothesis × Evidence Route × Audience/Culture × Offer/CTA × Media Form × Product Presence × Visual Mechanism`.

This is not a Cartesian quota. Create 0..N candidates where the intersection changes commercial value, creative mechanism, experiment variable, surface-native behavior or learning value.

## 4. A/B and variants
Same shopper question/surface may legitimately have multiple assets when a material variable differs: hook, evidence carrier, audience framing, offer, CTA, product presence, subject/action/scene, composition grammar, visual carrier, copy hierarchy, motion/beat, creator performance, or another testable mechanism.

Do not call such variants decorative duplicates. Preserve explicit Variant IDs, hypotheses, tested variable, metric and deployment pool.

## 5. Merge boundary
Never merge merely because question, strategic job, family or surface match. Merge only if there is no meaningful difference in information, creative/experiment hypothesis, audience/offer treatment, evidence carrier, product role, scene/action, visual mechanism, media form or platform-native behavior.

## 6. FULL materialization
FULL delivery exposes:
- total candidate count;
- family/surface coverage;
- deployment pools;
- current Activation Set capacity and selected count;
- explicit reasons for omitted relevant families;
- all production-ready candidate Design Cards and four production objects.

Live activation metadata is not permission to omit Test/Rotation/Paid/A+/PDP/Social/Creator/Video production objects.

## 7. Failure conditions
FAIL when any of these occurs:
- `total_asset_candidates` is truncated to gallery/video capacity by slicing, top-N selection or host authoring;
- only Live/Gallery assets are materialized while activated Paid/A+/PDP/Social/Creator/Video/Test/Rotation opportunities disappear;
- A/B hypotheses are merged because core information is the same;
- the authoring objective says `smallest sufficient`, `fewest assets possible` or equivalent minimization for the Asset Universe;
- platform slot count becomes a global asset count;
- family/surface omissions are silent rather than explicit decisions.

## 8. Product authority
The Agent remains the curator. This contract does not force every family to exist and does not impose 20/30/40 assets. It prevents artificial shrinking and requires complete opportunity discovery before activation pruning.
