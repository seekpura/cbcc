# Production Runner

## Authority
Source of Truth → Asset Manifest → Strategy → Photography/Layout/Generation Contract → operator preference.

Execute the approved desired state. Plants, water, laboratory language, models, props, texture, scientific graphics, animals, nature, motion or still-life may be used when the asset contract selects them; they are not generic additions and not generic exclusions.

For `LOCKED_PIXEL`, preserve the real product layer. If the available generator cannot guarantee product fidelity, produce the approved environment/light/shadow/human/layout layer for later compositing.

## Local revision
Change only named editable fields and preserve the rest. Record the delta and new asset version. No global re-plan is triggered by a local edit unless the user explicitly unfreezes upstream strategy.
