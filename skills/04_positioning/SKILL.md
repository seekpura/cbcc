# Positioning & Action Strategy

## Required outputs
- positioning statement;
- primary conversion proposition (one primary proposition for first-screen memory);
- 3–5 secondary reasons-to-buy;
- trust drivers / objection reducers / proof / emotional value;
- P0/P1/P2 information hierarchy with rationale;
- 4P and conversion-funnel decisions where relevant;
- RACE Action handoff linking Research findings to Listing/Visual/Communication actions.

Do not mechanically fill frameworks; every entry explains why it changes a real decision.
