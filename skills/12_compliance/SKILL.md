# Compliance

Three layers: PRODUCT COMPLIANCE / CLAIM COMPLIANCE / CREATIVE COMPLIANCE.

P0 categories include regulated beauty/medical/food/supplement/infant/financial/electrical/certification/religious-certification contexts. Unknown current local law = REGULATORY_CHECK_REQUIRED, never guessed.

Visuals are claims: water, lab/doctor, ingredient plants, results, badges, certifications, included accessories and before/after imagery can imply facts. Apply Visual Semantics Gate rather than blanket aesthetic bans.

Output: compliance_register.json, claim_decisions.json, visual_semantics_decisions.json, platform_rule_gaps.json, P0/P1/P2 findings.
