# Video / Creator

No fixed 15s/30s requirement. Duration and format depend on platform, placement, consumer question and funnel stage.

Formats may include Product Demo, Problem/Solution, Routine, Education, Comparison, Lifestyle, FAQ, Objection Handling and Review-style only when clearly presented as creative format rather than fabricated customer testimony.

Each video defines: objective, audience, hook, consumer question, script, shot list, product source/fidelity, visual style, voice, subtitles, CTA, duration, platform/placement, claims, culture check and music-rights requirement.

AI people must not be presented as real customers giving fabricated testimonials.
